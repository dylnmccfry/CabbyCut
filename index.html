How it works

A frameless, European-style box of 3/4" plywood, joined with glue and pocket screws.

  1. Pick a cabinet. Base, wall or tall, or start from a preset: base cabinet, sink base, vanity, wall cabinet or pantry.
  2. Enter the size. Width, height and depth in inches. Fractions like 34 1/2 are fine.
  3. Choose the front. A door (or a pair over 24" wide) on concealed hinges, or 1 to 8 drawers on full-extension slides.
  4. Build your plans. Cut list, sheet layouts, part drawings, build steps and shopping list, ready on your phone or as a PDF.

Safety guidelines

Please read these before you cut or install anything.

CabbyCut is a planning guide, not professional advice. Check every measurement before you cut, follow your tools' safety instructions, and fasten every cabinet to wall studs. You build and install at your own risk.

Check before you cut

  • Plans are calculated only from the sizes you enter. Measure your space, your plywood and your hardware, and check the numbers before cutting.
  • Plywood thickness varies. Measure it, and make a test cut or dry-fit before cutting expensive sheets.
  • Check hinge and drawer slide sizes and instructions against the hardware you actually buy.

Use power tools safely

  • Read and follow the manual for every tool you use, including all guards, riving knives and push sticks.
  • Wear eye and hearing protection, and use dust collection or a dust mask.
  • Never cut small parts freehand. Support large sheets, and get help handling full sheets.
  • If a cut or operation feels unsafe, or you haven't been trained on it, don't do it. Find another method or ask someone experienced.

Hang wall cabinets securely

  • Fasten wall cabinets into wall studs with cabinet screws. Never hang a cabinet from drywall alone or from drywall anchors.
  • CabbyCut does not calculate how much weight a cabinet, shelf or wall can hold. Don't overload shelves, and keep heavy items in base cabinets.
  • Masonry, plaster, metal studs and uneven walls need different fasteners and methods. Get professional help if you're not sure.
  • Always have a helper when lifting and hanging a cabinet.

Prevent tip-overs

  • Fasten tall cabinets and drawer cabinets to the wall before loading them.
  • Children can climb open drawers and doors. Anchor every floor cabinet and consider anti-tip hardware.

Watch for wires and pipes

  • Check for electrical wiring, plumbing and gas lines before drilling or screwing into a wall.
  • Leave plumbing, electrical and gas work to licensed professionals, and follow your local building codes.

Finishes and dust

  • Follow the finish maker's instructions and work with good ventilation.
  • Rags soaked with oil-based finishes can catch fire on their own. Lay them flat to dry outdoors or store them in a sealed metal container of water.
